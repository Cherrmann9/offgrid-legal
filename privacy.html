OffGrid Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

OffGrid is a camping spot tracker and trip planner. It is designed to keep your data on your device.

What OffGrid stores

  • Your camping spots, including names, categories, notes, coordinates, and any photos you attach.
  • Your trips, including names, dates, stops, departure and return points, and your vehicle's fuel economy.
  • Favorites and app settings.

All of this is stored only on your iPhone. OffGrid has no user accounts, no servers of its own, and never uploads your spots, trips, photos, or settings anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Location

OffGrid asks for permission to use your location while the app is open. Your location is used to center the map on you, sort your spots by distance, and plan trips that start from where you are. Your location is processed on the device and is not sent to the developer.

Third-party services the app contacts

To draw maps and load information, OffGrid connects to these services. Each receives the standard information any internet request carries, such as your IP address and the map area being requested.

  • Apple Maps for the base map, search results, and driving directions. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Apple Weather for the forecast shown at each trip stop. The stop's coordinates are sent to Apple to get the forecast. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • USGS Elevation Point Query Service to fill in a spot's elevation from its coordinates when you create it.
  • OpenTopoMap for topographic map tiles, only when you turn on the Topography layer or download an area for offline use.
  • Wikimedia Commons for park photos.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive on, OffGrid keeps a copy of your spots, trips, favorites, and photos in your own iCloud Drive so they appear on your other devices. This uses your Apple account only; the developer has no access to it. Turn off iCloud Drive for OffGrid in Settings to keep everything on one device.

Backups

The Back Up option writes a file you control, containing your spots, trips, favorites, and photos, to a location you choose. Restore reads such a file back in.

When you tap Depart or Directions, OffGrid opens Apple Maps or Google Maps with your route. From that point the other app's privacy policy applies.

What OffGrid does not do

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
  • No data collection by the developer.

Children

OffGrid is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
